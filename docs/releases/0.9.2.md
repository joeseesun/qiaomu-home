主页更跟手：关掉硬件加速也能满帧滚动。

- 毛玻璃卡片不再每帧实时模糊：壁纸只模糊一次，滚动交给显示层，外观不变。
- 刷新、切换标签、搜索输入不再闪烁或卡顿；后台的主页标签不再每秒重绘。
- 搜索在大库里更快，结果顺序不变。

### English

- Frosted cards no longer re-blur the wallpaper on every frame: Home scrolls at full frame rate even with hardware acceleration off, and looks the same.
- Refreshing, switching tabs and typing in search no longer flicker or stutter; background Home tabs stop repainting every second.
- Search is faster in large vaults, with the same result order.

Validation: repository checks, plus an isolated Obsidian 1.14.5 with GPU disabled on a Retina display: scrolling went from 34 dropped frames over 2.3 s to none over 0.82 s; typing, refresh and tab switches dropped no frames; screenshots match the previous glass (mean pixel difference under 0.2/255). Resize, sidebar toggle, dim change, no wallpaper, page switch and search were re-checked. Phones keep the previous live blur; physical mobile devices and pop-out windows were not tested in this release.
